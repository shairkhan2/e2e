Create a room

Share the link. They tap it and they're in — no account, no app. Messages and audio are end-to-end encrypted with a key that never leaves the link.

  • The room key is generated in your browser and lives after the # in the link — browsers never send that part to a server.
  • The server relays ciphertext. It cannot read your messages, hear your call, or see nicknames.
  • Nothing is stored. The room disappears when it expires, and so does its history.
  • You can change the expiry any time while you're in the room.
