How it stays private

The key is made on your phone and lives in the link itself — after the #, the part browsers never send to a server.

So the server passes along scrambled bytes it can't read. Your messages and calls only make sense on the devices that have the link.

Anyone holding the link can join, so send it somewhere you trust.
